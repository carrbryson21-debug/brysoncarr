Methods to catch fish

Now I know what you might be thinking: catching fish is easy; you just use a rod and reel, right? But actually, there are multiple ways to catch fish depending on your type of rod and reel- whether that is a spinning baitcasting or a fly-fishing rod and reel, and depending on the way you fish, there are multiple ways to use each one

Spinning rod and reel

The spinning reel is a fishing rod and reel that is mainly used for casting lures and baits that are lighter and a low profile type of lure and is more user-friendly than some of the other rods that will be discussed later. Such as the baitcaster and fly rod

Baitcasting rod and reel

The baitcasting rod and reel is a type of rod and reel that is actually used for heavier baits and has more accuracy than all the other rods because of the way you cast with it. See the photo of the rod and reel and observe how you can touch and essentially thumb the line to have more precision when casting. This really helps you cast where you want to cast, which in turn helps you to catch fish and be a more successful fisherman or fisherwoman

fly rod and reel

The fly rod and reel are a very unique type of fishing rod and reel, as instead of using the weight of the lure to cast it instead uses the weight of the line to cast and this only works because fly rods have a special type of line and due to this , the fly rod can use really light lures; examples of lures are flies like lures and grub-like lures.
