Best Lures To fish

this is a really hard question to answer about fishing because there is no one universal answer to this specfic question about the best lures and because of this I would like to give GUIDELINES to lures that have a higher chance of working than other lures during certain seasons to increase your chances of catching fish

Summer

the best lures in the summer really are more insect like ones such as worms and frogs and small fish these lure function great as fish during this period have a lot of activity and eat a lot

Fall

now during the fall the best lure to use is a crank bait or square bill crank bait and any lure that can really get down deep this is due to the way that when the weather temperature drops the fish go deeper and deeper in the water column and this is a way to still catch them.

Winter

as for winter the best baits for fish here are lures that are really slow and small you want to use a lure that can be worked super slow such as the blade bait as the fish themselves are gonna be looking for an easy meal where they don't have to expand any evergy to get said food and if you can make it like that you increase your odds of catching fish in what is arguably the hardest time of the year to catch them

Spring

spring time is the most exciting part of the year when it comes to fishing as the water slowly warms up from the winter the bass get more aggressive and due to this you can usually at the start of the spring will fish sort of like you do in winter meaning you need slow and very real like presentation like a senko, but as it fully warms up, you can essentially fish with any lure you want too!!
