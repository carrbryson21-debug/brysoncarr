Where to find fish???

Ah, the age-old question of where to find fish. Now, you might think this is quite obvious: they are in the water, right? And while that is true, where specifically in the water column are they? That is what this page aims to discuss and explain

What are water columns

Now, in my earlier passage, I explained that the goal of this page is to find fish in the water column. But what is a water column? Well, a water column is a vertical layer of water with varying amounts of light and temperature, and for fishing, this really matters, as fish, depending on the time of year, will be in different parts of the water column, and if you are fishing in a different water column than them at that time; you are not going to catch fish. Where and when to fish certain water columns

Now, this is a very tricky question because it honestly varies when you should fish or not fish a certain water column, as it really depends on the temperature and the time of year. For example, in the summer during the morning you would want to fish the top of the water column as the fish are shallow, but as the day progresses you would want to fish deeper as they travel deeper. The main idea of fishing the water column, regarding when and where to, is that you should aim to fish the deep when it is really hot and cold and finally the thermocline zone when it is medium temperature such as 60-80 degrees to help understand this better I have attached image showing when you should fish certain water columns
